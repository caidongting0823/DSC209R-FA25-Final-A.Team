01 — Timeline

Three Years of Seismic Activity

From June 1988 to June 1990, thousands of earthquakes shook the region. The mainshock on October 17, 1989 stands out dramatically.

Loading timeline data...

The 6.9 magnitude mainshock released over 25 times more energy than all other recorded events in this period combined, accounting for about 96% of the total seismic energy.
02 — Scale

A Sea of Small Quakes,
One Giant

Most earthquakes are barely noticeable. But the distribution reveals
how rare and powerful the mainshock truly was.

Loading magnitude data...

25,466 earthquakes were magnitude 2.0 or less — small tremors often unfelt by humans. Only 75 exceeded magnitude 4.0, and just 7 reached magnitude 5.0 or higher.
03 — Impact

The Dramatic Shift:
Before & After

The earthquake didn't just strike once — it triggered thousands of aftershocks
that continued for months.

Loading comparison data...

In the 48 hours after the mainshock, there were 1,576 earthquakes — around 37 times more than in the 48 hours before it.
