History in Motion

1989 Loma Prieta Earthquake

A scrollable, data-driven look at how one evening in October 1989 reshaped the Bay Area.

This project combines USGS earthquake records, interactive maps, and historical media to trace the months of foreshocks, the 6.9 main shock, and the long tail of aftershocks that followed.
